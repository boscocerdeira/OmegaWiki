OmegaWiki simplifies complex research tasks. These tools assist in the daily handling of your digital assets:

Use the search bar to find answers across your entire database. The AI looks for the meaning behind your query, not just matching words. It returns results based on the context of your previous work.

## 🔍 Frequently Asked Questions

### Does the software send my files to the cloud?
No. OmegaWiki processes your data locally on your machine. Your notes remain on your personal computer drive.

### Can I run this without an internet connection?
The app needs an internet connection for the initial setup and to download the AI models. Once downloaded, you can use the core features offline.

### How do I update to a new version?
OmegaWiki checks for updates when you start the program. If a new version exists, a window appears with a prompt to update. Click accept to download the latest features.

### Is my research data private?
Your files stay offline. The local processing engine keeps your sensitive information private. OmegaWiki does not share your notes with external companies.

### What file types does the app support?
OmegaWiki supports text files, Markdown files, and common document formats. You can drag and drop these into the main window to add them to your wiki.

## 🛡️ Troubleshooting

If you face issues during the launch, check these common fixes:

*   Restart the application: Close OmegaWiki and open it again. This clears temporary memory errors.
*   Check for updates: Use the GitHub repository page to see if a newer version addresses your issue.
*   Verify permissions: Ensure you have write access to the folder where you store your research files.
*   Hardware acceleration: If the interface stutters, go to settings and disable hardware acceleration.

## 💡 Best Practices

Keep your folder structure flat. You do not need deep subfolders because the AI manages the connections for you. Use one main folder for your research collection. This makes it easier for the software to track your content.

Add notes in your own words. The AI performs better when it processes your original thought process. If you find a link that does not fit, remove it by using the delete key in the edit menu. This helps the AI learn your research habits.

Regularly back up your primary wiki folder. While OmegaWiki manages the structure, copies of your original files are important for peace of mind. Use an external drive or a standard backup service for these files.
